Noun

time (countable and uncountable, plural times)

  1. (uncountable) The inevitable progression into the future with the passing of present and past events. quotations ▼
    Time stands still for no one.
    • Come what come may, / Time and the hour runs through the roughest day.
      — William Shakespeare, “The Tragedie of Macbeth” (1606)
  2. A quantity of availability in time. synonym ▲
    Synonym: period
